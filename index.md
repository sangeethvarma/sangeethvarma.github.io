I am a researcher interested in questions about how information and knowledge relate to society and development.

I'm currently a PhD candidate in the School of Social Sciences at the [National Institute of Advanced Studies (NIAS)](https://www.nias.res.in/People/facility-member-info/Doctoral%20Students/Sangeeth%20Varma), Bengaluru, working under the supervision of [Dr. Anant Kamath](https://www.nias.res.in/People/facility-member-info/Faculty/Anant%20Kamath). My research sits at the intersection of Science and Technology Studies and development studies — broadly, I'm interested in how knowledge, expertise, and infrastructure shape (and are shaped by) contested visions of progress.

My doctoral work, tentatively titled *"Developmental Publics: Infrastructure, Politics, and Contested Visions of Progress in Keralam,"* looks at how Kerala's much-studied development trajectory. I wish to explore qualitative changes that have happened in the development discourse and development policies. Using the shelved K-Rail SilverLine project as an empirical site, I'm trying to understand how what I call "Developmental Publics" — the multiple, shifting collectivities that form around competing ideas of infrastructure, welfare, ecology, and progress — negotiate, contest, and reshape state-led development.

I trained first in Physics and Electrical and Electronics Engineering at BITS Pilani, K.K. Birla Goa Campus. I also worked briefly in the Quantum Transport lab at IISER, Thiruvananthapuram on the automation of multi-gated device measurements — work that led to a [publication on 2D superconductivity](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42005-018-0091-7).

I found myself more drawn to the social and political questions beneath the technical ones — how knowledge gets made, contested, and put to use — which eevntually took me through a Master's in Political Science (IGNOU) and then Development Studies at TISS Mumbai, where my dissertation examined the Kerala Shastra Sahitya Parishad, a people's science movement, and how it responded to the COVID-19 pandemic.

Along the way I've also worked on climate-smart agriculture policy at the Council on Energy, Environment and Water (CEEW)
